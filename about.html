About ARCF

Dedicated to advancing the art and science of respiratory care since 1989

Our Story

The American Respiratory Care Foundation (ARCF) was established in 1989 as a nonprofit organization dedicated to promoting respiratory health through research, education, and patient-focused philanthropic activities.

For over three decades, we have been at the forefront of supporting respiratory care professionals and advancing the field through grants, scholarships, and educational initiatives. Our mission is to improve respiratory health and the quality of life for individuals with respiratory conditions worldwide.

The ARCF works closely with respiratory therapists, physicians, researchers, and educators to foster innovation, support professional development, and improve patient outcomes.

Our Mission

To promote respiratory health through research, education, and patient-focused philanthropy that advances the art and science of respiratory care.

Our Vision

To be the premiere organization supporting respiratory research, education, and care worldwide, and to ensure optimal respiratory health for all.
